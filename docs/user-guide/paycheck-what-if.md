# Paycheck what-if

**What it answers:** what happens to my cheque if I change a deduction?

Take a real pay date and change one thing: raise your 401(k) percentage, add an HSA contribution,
change your W-4. It recalculates the whole cheque and shows the difference side by side.

## Why it is separate from [Paycheck](paycheck.md)

The Paycheck screen tells you what will actually happen. This one is a sandbox — nothing here is
saved, and your income source is untouched.

## Reading the result

Both cheques are shown in full, with the change highlighted. The number to look at is usually not the
deduction itself but the **net**: putting another $100 into a pre-tax deduction costs you less than
$100 of take-home, because it reduces what is withheld. The app shows exactly how much less.

Remember that a 401(k) deferral reduces income tax but **not** Social Security and Medicare, while a
Section 125 deduction (health premiums, HSA through payroll) reduces both. The formulas on each line
make this visible.
