# Loans

**What it answers:** what do I owe, what is it costing, and when is it gone?

## Recording a balance

The **＋** on each row records a balance with its date. Do this when a statement arrives.

**A loan's balance is whatever you last recorded**, not a calculation. Extra payments and timing move
the real figure off any schedule, so the lender is the authority. Until you record one, the app shows
the original principal marked **orig**, so you can tell a real figure from a stand-in.

## Projection

From the latest balance: the payoff date, total interest remaining, and what extra principal would
save. It also shows what the original schedule says the balance *should* be now, next to the actual —
the gap tells you whether you are ahead or behind.

## Linking to a budget line and an asset

- **Budget line** — the monthly payment belongs in [Budget](budget.md) like any other bill.
- **Asset** — attach the mortgage to the house on [Assets](assets.md) and it shows equity.
