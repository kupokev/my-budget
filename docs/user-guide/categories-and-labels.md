# Categories & Labels

**What it answers:** what kind of spending was it, and where did it happen?

Two words describe a transaction:

- **Category** — what kind of thing it was: Utilities, Groceries, Travel.
- **Label** — where it happened: Amazon, Costco, IHG, Hilton.

A label belongs to a category. The same name can appear under two categories and mean different
things: *Gas* under Automotive is the pump, *Gas* under Utilities is the bill.

## Why both

Because "General merchandise at Amazon" and "General merchandise at Costco" are the same kind of
spending in different places, and you may want to budget or earn rewards on one without the other.
Labels let you split a category without inventing a category per merchant.

## Editing

Both tables save as you go — change a name, tick a box, pick a category, and it saves when the field
loses focus. There is no Save button.

The list does not re-sort while you are editing it; ordering settles next time you open the page.

## Deleting

A category in use cannot be deleted, and the app tells you what is using it — budget lines,
transactions, labels, import rules, card earn rules, goals. Untick **Active** instead to take it out
of the pickers while keeping the history intact.

## Planned spending is not set here

The figures shown are read back from [Budget](budget.md). A category's monthly figure is the sum of
its budget lines; it is not something you type here.
