# Owed to me

**What it answers:** who owes me money, and are they paying?

For money you front on someone else's behalf — a shared bill, a loan to family — and expect back.

## How it works

1. Add a **person**.
2. Add what they owe: either a **share of a budget line** (they pay half the phone bill every month)
   or a **one-off charge**.
3. Record **payments** as they come in, and allocate each one to the months it covers.

## Reading it

Each person shows what has been charged, what has been paid, and the balance. A recurring obligation
keeps accruing each month until you end it, so an unpaid month shows up rather than quietly
disappearing.

## Why allocate a payment to months

Because "they paid $200" is ambiguous when they owe $50 a month and are three months behind.
Allocating tells you whether they have caught up or are still two months short.
