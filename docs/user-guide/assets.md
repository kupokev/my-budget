# Assets

**What it answers:** what is the house and the car worth, less what is owed against them?

## Recording a value

An asset is a home or a vehicle — something whose value you have to state, because nothing fetches
it. Record a value with its date; record another one later and the app keeps both. The history shows
each valuation and the change from the one before.

Recording the same date twice replaces that entry rather than adding a second.

## Attaching a loan

Attach the mortgage to the house and the car loan to the car. The asset then shows **equity**: the
latest value less the outstanding balance, with the formula.

Loans come from [Loans](loans.md); attaching one here does not change the loan, it just says what it
is secured against.

## Why values are manual

The app uses no paid valuation service, so nothing guesses what your house is worth. Take the figure
from a source you trust, record it with the date, and the net-worth history will use the value that
applied at each point in time rather than today's number applied backwards.
