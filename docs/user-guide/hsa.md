# HSA

**What it answers:** how much can I put in this year, and am I on pace?

## Eligibility by month

HSA limits are not annual in practice — they depend on which months you were eligible and whether
your cover was self-only or family. Set each month, and the app works out your actual limit rather
than assuming a whole year.

The last-month rule and the catch-up contribution for 55 and over are both handled; the limit
calculation shows its working:

```
$8,300.00 family × 7/12 + $4,150.00 self-only × 5/12 = $6,570.83
```

## Contributions

Record what has gone in: payroll deductions, employer contributions, and anything paid directly.
Employer money counts toward the limit, which is the part people forget.

## Pace

The planner shows what you would need per month, and per remaining paycheck, to reach your target.
A goal with the HSA metric on [Goals](goals.md) will warn you on the Dashboard when you fall behind.

## Where the money sits

An HSA is an account like any other — add it on [Accounts](accounts.md) with type *Health Savings
Account*. If it is invested, the holdings belong on [Investments](investments.md), and the account's
value is then taken from what it holds.
