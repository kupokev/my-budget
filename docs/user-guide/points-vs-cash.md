# Points vs cash

**What it answers:** should I pay for this hotel night with points or with money?

Enter what the stay would cost in cash, and what it costs in points. It tells you the value you are
getting per point and whether that is a good deal.

## The bands

| Value per point | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Under $0.44 | Poor — pay cash |
| $0.46 – $0.54 | Fair |
| $0.55 and above | Good — use points |

These are deliberately the same for every program. A point is only worth what a specific booking
gives you, and the same program can be poor value one night and excellent the next, so an average
per-program figure would mislead more than it helps.

## Two corrections worth making

- **Taxes.** A points booking usually wipes out the taxes too, so compare against the cash price
  *including* tax — otherwise you understate what the points saved.
- **Forgone earning.** Paying cash earns points and may count toward status; paying with points does
  not. Subtract what you would have earned before deciding.

## Buying points

If you buy points to top up a booking, the price you pay for them is the floor on what they are
worth. Buying at 0.5¢ and redeeming at 0.6¢ is a thin margin; buying during a 100% bonus changes the
arithmetic considerably. Work out the purchase price per point first, then compare.
