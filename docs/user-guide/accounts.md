# Accounts

**What it answers:** where does money sit, and is there enough in the right places?

One row per account: checking, savings, HSA, brokerage, retirement.

## Balances

Record a balance with the **⇄** icon. The same form handles three things: a starting or current
balance, a transfer in, or a transfer out. For a transfer between your own accounts, pick the other
account — the matching entry is written there too, and the pair deletes together.

**An investment account is not given a typed balance.** It is worth what it holds, so its value comes
from [Investments](investments.md) automatically.

## This month

What is due to come out of each account this month, from the budget lines funded by it. Expand an
account to see each line, its monthly accrual and the formula behind it.

This is the figure that tells you whether a transfer is needed, and it is why **Funded from** on a
budget line matters: a bill paid by credit card still counts against the account that will really pay
for it, not the card.

## Rainy-day fund

Tick **Part of the rainy-day fund** on any account and it counts toward the months-of-expenses figure
on the [Dashboard](dashboard.md). A brokerage or a T-Bill account counts if you would actually use
it.

## Account numbers

Stored in full and masked on screen, with a **show** link. This is a local, single-user app; the full
number is there when you need it to identify an account.

## Minimum balance

The floor you want to keep. The app warns when an account's balance will not cover what is due in the
next fortnight while staying above it.
