# Tax tables

**What it answers:** what figures is the paycheck maths using?

Federal brackets, standard deductions, Social Security wage base, Medicare thresholds, Missouri
brackets, and HSA contribution limits — one set per year.

## Why you can see and edit them

Because [Paycheck](paycheck.md) claims to match your real stub, and a claim like that is only
trustworthy if you can check what went into it. Every withholding line links back to the figures on
this screen.

## Verified

Each year is marked verified or not. Unverified means the figures were carried forward or estimated
before the official numbers were published, and the paycheck estimate says so at the top rather than
presenting a guess as fact.

When the real figures come out, update the year and mark it verified.

## Adding a year

Year-keyed tables are additive — a new year is added without touching the old ones, so a cheque from
two years ago is still calculated with the rules that applied then.
