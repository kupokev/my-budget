## What usually explains a gap

- A benefit amount changed (open enrollment) and the election has no end date / new row.
- 401(k) is a percent of gross but the plan caps at the annual limit late in the year.
- The employer uses the aggregate method for a bonus, or withholds on a different pay-period count.
- Missouri table or standard deduction differs from the shipped provisional 2026 values.
